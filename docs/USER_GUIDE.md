# SmartPocket User Guide for Students

Welcome to **SmartPocket** — your personal student budget, pocket money, and savings advisor.

---

## 1. Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Sign In or Register
1. Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
2. If this is your first time, click **Register** and enter your name, college email, and password.
3. If running development demo data, sign in with:
   - **Email:** `student@example.edu`
   - **Password:** `Password123!`

---

## 2. Managing Pocket Money & Spending

### Adding Your Pocket Money / Allowance
1. Click **+ Add Transaction** in the top bar.
2. Enter the amount you received from your parents, part-time job, or stipend.
3. Select **Transaction Type:** `Income / Allowance`.
4. Classification will automatically be set to `Income`.
5. Click **Save Transaction**.
6. Your **Available Pocket Cash** and **Safe Daily Spending Allowance** will update immediately!

### Recording an Expense
1. Click **+ Add Transaction**.
2. Enter the amount (e.g. `₹150.00`).
3. Select **Transaction Type:** `Expense`.
4. Choose the classification:
   - **Essential Need (50%):** Mess food, groceries, textbooks, bus/metro commute, medicine.
   - **Discretionary Want (30%):** Cafes, dining out, movies, gaming, shopping.
5. Select the category and enter an optional note (e.g. "Canteen lunch").
6. Click **Save Transaction**.

---

## 3. Understanding Your Safe Daily Spending Allowance

The green highlight banner at the top of your dashboard shows your **Safe Daily Spending Allowance**.

### How is it calculated?
SmartPocket divides your spendable money by the remaining days in the month (including today).

- **Healthy Budget:** You are spending within your safe pace.
- **Near Limit:** You have used more than 85% of your budget or daily pace is tight.
- **Budget Exceeded:** You've spent more than your monthly limit. Focus strictly on essentials.

---

## 4. 50/30/20 Student Budget Rule

SmartPocket tracks your spending against the proven 50/30/20 guideline:
- **50% Needs:** Must-have essentials for student living.
- **30% Wants:** Lifestyle and social spending.
- **20% Savings:** Reserved for your savings goals.

You can customize these targets under the **Budgets & 50/30/20** tab to match your personal situation.

---

## 5. Savings Goals

1. Navigate to **Savings Goals**.
2. Click **+ New Goal** (e.g. "Semester Textbooks" target ₹3,000).
3. Whenever you save some pocket money, click **+ Deposit**.
4. SmartPocket records the deposit and deducts the cash from your spendable pocket balance, keeping your accounting consistent!

---

## 6. Asking the Pocket AI Advisor

Click on the **Pocket AI Advisor** tab. You can ask questions such as:
- *"Can I afford ₹800 shoes?"*
- *"How much can I spend today?"*
- *"Why is my budget running low?"*
- *"Am I spending too much on wants?"*
- *"How much have I spent on food this month?"*

The advisor works **100% offline** without needing an internet connection or external API keys!

---

## 7. Installing as a PWA

On mobile (Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS):
1. Tap the browser menu (`⋮` or Share icon).
2. Tap **Add to Home screen** / **Install App**.
3. SmartPocket will open in a full-screen native app experience and work even with airplane mode enabled!
